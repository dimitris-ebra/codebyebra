The platform

Visitors can explore the teacher’s academic background, download teaching resources and find tutoring information, in a design that puts clarity, trust and accessibility first. Integrated contact options (email, phone and social links) and remote-learning support via Zoom keep communication with students simple.

What I learned

My first freelance opportunity came from my economics teacher, who trusted me to build and maintain a web application for their needs. It meant working as both a developer and a service provider: communicating with a client, gathering requirements, meeting deadlines and delivering revisions without losing sight of the bigger picture.

Technically, I combined Python (Flask) with JavaScript into a responsive full-stack application, implemented secure authentication, and protected it against common vulnerabilities like XSS and SQL injection. I learned to manage the database carefully through major updates so no data was ever lost, and I took ownership of hosting, deploying and monitoring the app in production — where I still keep it stable today.
